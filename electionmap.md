### Spatial Problem
Visualizing 2020 county-level election results while maintaining a clean visual hierarchy that accurately represents various margin criteria.

### GIS Methodology
* **Thematic Mapping:** Developed a **Diverging Color Scale** (Red to Blue) to represent the strength of the political shift in each county.
* **Visual Hierarchy:** Established a clear hierarchy using labels and line weights to ensure county boundaries remained legible without cluttering the regional view.
* **Cartographic Elements:** Included all standard map elements: a dynamic legend, North arrow, and scale bar calibrated to the map's projection.
* **Data Integrity:** Cleaned and joined the 2020 Presidential election data from [Source Name] to the US County shapefile.
